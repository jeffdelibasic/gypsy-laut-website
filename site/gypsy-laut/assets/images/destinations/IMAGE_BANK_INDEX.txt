GYPSY LAUT — DESTINATION IMAGE BANK

Purpose
This folder contains temporary, licensed destination scenery for future pages,
backgrounds and editorial transitions. It is separate from real Gypsy Laut
vessel photography and from the Private Day Charter image bank.

FOLDER GUIDE

lembongan-penida
Local island context: Nusa Lembongan, its mangroves and working-water life,
plus Nusa Penida coastlines. Best for local destination storytelling, Stay
Aboard context and day-charter transitions.

lombok-gili-islands
Lombok coast and Northern Gili Islands: coastline, water texture, small-boat
context and island aerials. Best for Private Expeditions, Surf & Paddle and
reef/water supporting imagery.

sumbawa-moyo
Sumbawa coast, surf, sunset and Moyo's Mata Jitu waterfall. Best for longer
private-expedition stories, remote-island transitions and wider-region pages.

komodo
Padar, Komodo coast and anchorage landscapes. Best for Go Further, premium
expedition heroes and dramatic full-width geography.

USAGE GUARDRAILS
- Never present a third-party vessel in these photos as Gypsy Laut.
- Prefer real Gypsy Laut photography whenever the vessel itself is the subject.
- Use wide aerial and coastline images for full-width sections; use the smaller
boat, seaweed and water-texture images as supporting/editorial crops.
- Keep grading warm and natural. Avoid HDR, artificial turquoise or glossy
resort treatment.
- Source pages and licence notes should be checked again before a paid campaign
or print use.
