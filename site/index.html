Your own place on the water.

Wake up on the water. Have coffee or breakfast on deck, then swim straight from the boat. Take the tender ashore for beaches, lunch or a wander around the island, then come back aboard for sunset, dinner and the night.

There’s space to slow down and settle in, with generous outdoor lounging, an indoor saloon, air conditioning, Starlink and flatscreen TVs. Surfboards and paddle boards are aboard when you feel like heading out. Provisioning can be tailored to your stay, and a private chef can be arranged.

Breakfast on deck. A swim before lunch. Cold drinks at sunset. Your own place on the water.

An outdoor projector and screen for movie nights on deck are planned.

Go further from here.

Gypsy Laut is the base. The tender gets you into the action.

Wake up in Mushroom Bay and decide where the day takes you. A beach on Lembongan, reefs around Penida, a surf session, a snorkel stop or lunch ashore — the tender makes the islands part of your backyard.

The 5.2m aluminium centre-console, with its 90hp Suzuki, links the boat to shore and sea — for island access, surf transfers, fishing runs, snorkelling and dive logistics.

On the Water

Wildlife & Water

Reefs, mantas and some of the most remarkable water around the islands.

Head out for snorkelling, reef exploring or a day on the water around Lembongan and Penida. Manta encounters may be possible when conditions allow, while scuba experiences can be arranged through selected premium local dive partners.

Seasonal mola experiences are for suitable scuba divers and depend on conditions.

Surf & Paddle

Chase waves, not schedules.

Use the tender to reach nearby breaks, head out with an experienced local surf guide, or grab one of the boards aboard when conditions line up. When the ocean is calmer, paddle boards give you another way to explore around the bay.

Surf when it’s on. Paddle when it’s calm. Come back to your own boat.

Fishing & Spearfishing

Fish the islands. Make the day your own.

From a relaxed fishing run to a more focused sportfishing day, the tender makes it easy to get onto the water. Longer fishing safaris can also form part of a private expedition. Curated spearfishing experiences may be arranged with an external specialist guide.

Catch it. Bring it back aboard. Make it part of dinner.

Fishing and spearfishing are subject to permitted areas and conditions.

Meet Gypsy Laut

More than two decades exploring Indonesian waters.

A new chapter from Lembongan.

Gypsy Laut has spent more than two decades exploring Indonesian waters. Today, after a full external and internal refurbishment, she begins a new chapter from Nusa Lembongan — as a private floating home, island base and platform for longer journeys through Indonesia.

At 65 feet, she has the space to settle in without losing the character of a real working vessel. Timber interiors, generous deck space, an indoor saloon, air conditioning and modern connectivity make life aboard comfortable, while her range, watermaker and tenders keep the focus firmly on life at sea.
